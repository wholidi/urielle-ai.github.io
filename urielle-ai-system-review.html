Our method

From uncertain assumptions to defensible technical decisions.

A practical, structured review with clear evidence boundaries.

01

Define the decision

Agree the intended use, architecture baseline, review questions, criteria, evidence access and exclusions.

02

Examine the evidence

Assess supplied diagrams, design documents, decision records, development controls and other in-scope material.

03

Challenge and classify

Record observations as evidence-backed findings, unverified assumptions or matters needing further testing. Prioritize business and technical consequences.

04

Recommend the next decision

Explain what to retain, simplify, validate through a pilot, defer or redesign—along with recommended actions and ownership.

Common questions

Know what is included.

Do you need access to our source code?

Not necessarily. A scoped advisory review can begin with architecture diagrams, design documents, decision records and delivery plans. Source-code or runtime assessment requires a separately agreed scope.

Is this an ISO/IEC 42001 certification audit?

No. This is an independent advisory or scoped assurance review. It is not an accredited certification audit or legal compliance opinion.

Can you review an AI vendor proposal before we sign?

Yes. We can assess technical assumptions, architecture feasibility, integration implications, delivery controls and proposed risk mitigations based on supplied evidence.

Will you implement the recommended changes?

The core service is an independent review. Implementation and engineering execution are separate activities, with roles and independence boundaries defined explicitly if considered.
